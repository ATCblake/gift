Document wallet::Pick a format that fits their existing paperwork without folding it. Keep important documents accessible rather than buried beneath decorative compartments.

Bag identifier::A distinctive tag helps distinguish similar bags. Give the recipient control over which contact details appear on the outside.

Replacement zipper pulls::These make a modest gift when a favorite bag is still serviceable. Match the attachment style and avoid excessively long dangling cords.
Compact notebook::Choose the paper size and binding they already like. A notebook that opens flat is easier to use at a desk or during study.
